
# AACE International PSP Certification Exam Guide

[![AACE International PSP](https://img.shields.io/badge/AACE%20International-PSP-005A9C?style=for-the-badge)](https://web.aacei.org/certification/certification-information/certifications-offered/professional-certifications) [![Certification Level](https://img.shields.io/badge/Level-Professional-1A3B5C?style=for-the-badge)](https://web.aacei.org/certification/certification-information/certifications-offered/professional-certifications)

A dedicated study guide and professional reference repository for candidates preparing for the **AACE International Planning & Scheduling Professional (PSP) Certification Exam**, covering project planning, integrated schedule development, monitoring, forecasting, analysis, and stakeholder communication.

---

## Exam Overview

The AACE International Planning & Scheduling Professional (PSP) certification recognizes experienced practitioners who can lead project planning and scheduling within the AACE Total Cost Management (TCM) framework and communicate effectively with project stakeholders.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based examination with multiple-choice and scenario questions, plus a written memo assignment.
- **Target Audience**: Experienced project planners, schedulers, project controls professionals, and practitioners responsible for integrated project schedules.
- **Prerequisites**: Candidates must meet AACE's education and industry-experience eligibility requirements. Review the current PSP Candidate Handbook for the applicable criteria.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Conduct Planning Duties

- Define project objectives, scope, deliverables, and the activities required to achieve project goals.
- Develop planning approaches that align resources, constraints, risks, and project requirements.
- Apply planning principles within the AACE Total Cost Management (TCM) framework.

### 2. Conduct Scheduling Duties

- Develop and maintain integrated project schedules using activity relationships, durations, calendars, constraints, and milestones.
- Apply critical path, float, progress measurement, schedule updating, and forecasting concepts.
- Analyze schedule performance, identify variances, evaluate impacts, and support corrective or recovery actions.

### 3. Communication Competency

- Interpret a workplace scenario and identify the key planning or scheduling issue.
- Organize a clear memo with a purpose statement, potential impacts, and actionable recommendations supported by rationale.
- Communicate conclusions professionally to project stakeholders using concise, structured writing.
